# About me
I am a 26-year-old first generation student from Santa Maria, California, pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science at the Colorado School of Mines. In 2023, I graduated from Cal Poly San Luis Obispo with a B.S. in Aerospace Engineering, after which I worked for two years in the commercial space launch industry as a flight safety analyst.

Through my work experience, I developed a passion for software engineering and decided to broaden my expertise with the goal of developing scalable systems for scientific applications.

## Research
I am currently a research assistant working with Dr. Daniel McKenzie in the Applied Mathematics and Statistics Department at Mines. My M.S. thesis research focuses on hybrid approaches to rainfall-runoff modelling, such as coupled architectures where neural networks parameterize the physical (and often differentiable) process-based hydrology models. These models have shown promise in both interpretability and predictive skill on ungauged basins and extreme events such as flood or drought.

In addition to my work at Mines, I also am a research software engineering intern for the National Lab of the Rockies, where I am writing software for energy-aware route planning.
